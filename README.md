`main.py` (standard library only) writes a single complete story about a journey,
told in exactly one sentence, to `entry.txt` inside `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(default `/output`), validating the contest gate first: 1–300 characters,
exactly one sentence, no title, no second sentence.

The story — chosen from 12 original candidates by a pairwise tournament scored
against the published judge brief, then tightened in a second revision pass:

> When the bridge washed out, the schoolteacher carried her pupils across the river
> on her back one by one, and the last child she carried grew up to be the engineer
> who rebuilt it thirty years later.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/output python3 main.py
```
